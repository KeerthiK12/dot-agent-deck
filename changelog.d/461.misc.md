Fixed `cargo xtask clean-e2e-tmp` refusing to reap e2e temp roots it could prove were dead. The tool judged every leftover `dad-tests-<pid>-*` root by age alone, so 280 roots (6.2 GB) with dead owning PIDs sat untouched because the oldest was under the 6-hour default — at a moment when the tmpfs was nearly full and swap had 5 MiB free. It now checks the owning PID first: a dead PID is reaped at any age, a live PID (including one this process can't signal) is never reaped regardless of age, and only a PID that can't be determined falls back to the age rule. This is developer tooling only — no change to the shipped binary.
