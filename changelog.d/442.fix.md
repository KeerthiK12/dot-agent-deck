## The selected deck card no longer fades into the idle ones

While you were typing into a pane, the selected card on the deck was drawn in a dimmed magenta — and on a dark terminal that lands in the same visual band as the dark gray an idle agent gets. The card you were pointed at became one of the hardest on screen to find, exactly when the deck was scenery rather than the thing you were driving. It was not only the outline: because the border style bleeds into the titles drawn over it, the card's name and its `Last:` / `Tools:` counters faded with it, so the selected card read as the faintest thing in the frame.

Selection is no longer a color at all. A deck card's border color now always means **status** — a selected agent that is working stays green, one waiting on you stays yellow, one that errored stays red — and selection is shown by **thickening the border** (`┃` instead of `│`) alongside the `▸ ` marker that was already there. Nothing is ever dimmed to indicate selection, in any mode; emphasis is only ever added.

Which mode you are in still reads off the same card. In command mode, where the keyboard drives the deck, the selected card's border is bold; while you are typing into a pane it drops back to normal weight, keeping its color, its thick border, and its `▸ ` marker. So "which card is selected" and "where are my keystrokes going" stay separate questions with separate answers, and neither is answered by making something harder to see.

This also gives the selected card its status back. Previously the accent color overwrote it and only the corner badge still said what the agent was doing; now the border keeps reporting status whether or not the card is selected. Deck cards now signal selection the same way embedded panes have always signalled focus — by border weight, not by spending another color.
